Clothing and accessories, chosen with care

Velloxa is an online store for everyday style. We bring clothing, footwear, watches, bags and more together in one place, at prices that make sense.

Our story

Velloxa started with a simple idea: good style should not be hard to find or hard to afford. Too many stores make you scroll through hundreds of products to find one you like.

So we keep our collection small and well chosen. Every product is picked for quality, comfort and value, and every page is built to be easy to browse.

What we stand for

Quality first

We check fabric, stitching and finish before a product goes on the shelf.

Fair prices

Clear prices and honest discounts, with no hidden charges at checkout.

Easy shopping

Simple filters, clear photos and quick delivery, so you find your style faster.

What you will find here

Men's and women's clothing, kids' wear, footwear, watches, bags, eyewear, jewellery, gadgets and beauty products.
